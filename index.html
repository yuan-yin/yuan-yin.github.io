Hi, I’m Yuan YIN 银元

AI Researcher at Valeo.ai

I work on parameter-efficient model adaptation, learning physical dynamics, and autonomous driving. My current research covers perception, motion forecasting, and decision making for self-driving, along with adapting large pretrained models and learning at scale. Across these, I favor methods that exploit the structure of a problem.

I defended my PhD on physics-aware deep learning in June 2023 at Sorbonne Université, supervised by Prof. Patrick Gallinari and Assoc. Prof. Nicolas Baskiotis. I hold a BSc in Computer Science from Beihang University and an MSc from Université Paris Cité and Sorbonne Université.

Research themes

  • Model adaptation

    Fine-tuning large pretrained models with few trainable parameters, and making learned models generalize to unseen conditions.

    • IPA
    • GEPS
    • CoDA
    • LEADS

  • Learning physical dynamics

    Combining physical models with deep networks, and forecasting PDEs continuously across space and time.

    • APHYNITY
    • DINo
    • CORAL

  • Autonomous driving

    Motion forecasting, end-to-end planning, and simulation for training and stress-testing driving policies.

    • Pictura
    • DrivoR
    • PPT
    • ReGentS
